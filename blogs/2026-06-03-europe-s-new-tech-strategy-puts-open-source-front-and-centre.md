The European Commission has just released a major strategy on technological sovereignty , and it’s placing open source software squarely in the spotlight. The message is clear: open source is key to true digital independence. Right now, Europe depends on non-EU tech providers for over 80% of critical digital infrastructure, and that’s a problem.
