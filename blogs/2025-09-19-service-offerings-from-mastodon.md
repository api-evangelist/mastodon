In a recent post , we shared that one of our priorities for 2025 is to establish a more sustainable financial base for the Mastodon organisation. In 2024, Mastodon gGmbH took over hosting of the European Commission’s Mastodon service . We also signed a support contract with the state of Schleswig-Holstein in Germany .
