Mastodon 4.4 is just around the corner: the release candidate is now available , and the final release should be coming along very soon. Just before the final version arrives, we wanted to shine a spotlight on some of the new features and changes that will be particularly important to anyone building apps or integrations. As a reminder, we also share a monthly engineering update (our Trunk and Tidbits series) and if you’ve been following those, some of these changes may already be familiar.
