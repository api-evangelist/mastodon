<p>We are happy to announce fiscal sponsorship via <a href="https://www.we-aid.org/en/project-details/106/">WE AID</a>, a German nonprofit organisation that supports charitable projects within an established legal framework. This move is part of <a href="https://blog.joinmastodon.org/2025/11/the-future-is-ours-to-build-together/">Mastodon’s transformation</a>.</p> <p>This partnership offers donors tax-deductible contributions in Germany and ensures verified integrity through WE AID’s oversight.
