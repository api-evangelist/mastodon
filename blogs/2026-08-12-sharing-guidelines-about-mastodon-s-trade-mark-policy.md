Recently, members of the community informed us that our Trade Mark Policy isn’t clear enough when it comes to when, where, and how servers and developers can use our brand name (Mastodon), particularly in the URL of a server. We heard you! Our team has worked to improve our Policy to make sure it is clear and fair to our community of server admins and developers.
